## Architecture Decision

**Party Pattern Benefits:**
- Single unified Contact model with role flags (is_customer, is_supplier)
- Contact can have multiple roles (both customer AND supplier)
- Consistent foreign key across Sales, Purchase, and Finance modules
- Simplified queries and reporting
- Standard ERP architecture pattern

## Validation Strategy

Validation moved to Request layer:
- ARInvoice validates contact is customer (is_customer = true)
- APInvoice validates contact is supplier (is_supplier = true)
- Clear Spanish error messages for invalid contacts
